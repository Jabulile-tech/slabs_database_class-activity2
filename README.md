2. Enterprise Information Systems Mapping


1. Transaction Processing System (TPS)
TPS is for daily operational work. At SuperMart SA, it records transactions that must be reliable and fast.

At checkout counters: scanning items, recording sales, quantity sold, price, discount, payment method (cash, card), customer loyalty points, printing receipt, and updating stock immediately after sale. Also handling returns and refunds.

At inventory warehouse: recording goods received from suppliers, goods issued to stores, stock levels, barcode updates, and stock count adjustments. If TPS fails, stock numbers will be wrong and customers may not find products. So it must be accurate and reliable.

2. Management Information Systems (MIS) & Decision Support Systems (DSS)
MIS is for store management to track performance. Store manager uses MIS to get weekly sales reports. For example, MIS will show total sales for the week, best selling products, worst selling products, sales per department, and compare this week vs last week. It takes data from TPS and makes it into summary reports. No calculation by manager, the system does it.

DSS helps when there is a problem like sudden drop in sales. DSS is interactive and helps with decision making. It can analyse what caused the drop, check if it is due to weather, competitor promotion, or stock shortage. It can also run what-if analysis. For example, if we run 20% promotion on groceries, what will happen to sales and profit? DSS will show predictions. So manager can decide whether promotion is worth it or not.

3. Executive Support Systems (ESS)
ESS is for CEO and top executives. It gives high level static reports and long term views.

CEO needs:
 Static reports: yearly profit, overall company performance, sales growth across all branches, financial summaries.
 Competitor data: market share compared to Shoprite, Pick n Pay, Woolworths, competitor prices and promotions.
 Long-term trend forecasts: future retail trends, customer buying behavior, forecast for next 5 years, where to open new stores, and economic trends like inflation effect on sales.
ESS uses graphs, dashboards and easy to read summaries, not detailed daily data.

4. Knowledge Work Systems (KWS) & Office Automation Systems (OAS)
KWS is for creating new knowledge, used by designers and analysts.

Example of KWS at SuperMart SA: Graphic designer uses Adobe Illustrator to design promotion posters, or Data analyst uses Power BI to analyse buying trends and create new product layouts.

OAS is for daily office admin work.

Example of OAS at SuperMart SA: 
    Microsoft Outlook for emails and meeting scheduling, Microsoft Excel for daily admin sheets, or Google Workspace for sharing documents. Also Teams or Zoom for meetings. These tools help with normal office tasks.

3. IT Landscape & Legacy Systems

Why connecting old legacy systems to new cloud apps increases complexity?
SuperMart SA has used old systems for 15 years and now also uses modern cloud apps. Old legacy systems were built with old technology and may not have APIs. New cloud apps use new technology. When we try to connect them, we need integration points. These integration points can cause security risks, because old systems have weak security. Also data formats are different, so we need to convert data. It also becomes hard to maintain because if one system fails, the whole chain fails. It also increases cost and time because we need middleware and extra consultants.

Two ways SuperMart SA can save costs using an IT Landscape Diagram:

1. End-of-Life asset tracking: IT Landscape Diagram shows all systems and when they were bought and when they will expire. So SuperMart SA can see which old computers, servers or software are at end of life and need replacement. This helps to plan budget and avoid sudden breakdown that costs more. Also can retire systems that are not used anymore and save license fees.

2. Standardization: Diagram shows all apps used in different branches. SuperMart SA may find that one branch uses one POS system and another branch uses different POS. With landscape diagram, they can see duplication. They can standardize to one system for all branches. This reduces training cost, support cost, and license cost. Also easier to integrate with cloud.
